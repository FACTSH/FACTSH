CHORDS — Archive of Moments

The
Visionary
Intervention.

CHORDS 2026 is a three-day international conference by the Department of Computational Science & Humanities, IIIT Kottayam, and the FACTS-H Lab. Centered on Digitization of Cultural Heritage, it addresses the urgent need to preserve cultural foundations facing environmental and urban threats.

Aligned with India's Gyan Bharatam initiative
Tools: Blender · RealityCapture · QGIS · Omeka
Hybrid format — in-person & online participation
Three days of keynotes, papers & workshops

Places to Visit

Adventure · Trekking ⭐ 4.6

Illikkal Kallu

📍 ~35 km · Poonjar

Dramatic hilltop monolith at 3,500 ft. Jeep rides, a short trek, and panoramic Western Ghats views. Misty mornings are unforgettable.

Open in Maps ↗
Waterfall ⭐ 4.6

Kattikkayam Waterfalls

📍 ~38 km · Poonjar

Multi-tiered cascade with a natural pool. Best June–September. Hidden near Illikkal Kallu — combine both in a single trip.

Open in Maps ↗
Backwaters · Cruise ⭐ 4.5

Kumarakom Backwaters

📍 ~16 km · Kumarakom

Shikara rides through coconut-fringed canals and Vembanad Lake. Bird sanctuary, houseboats, and village canals nearby.

Open in Maps ↗
Lake · Kayaking ⭐ 4.6

Vembanad Lake

📍 ~20 km · Kottayam–Alappuzha

India's longest lake. Kayak at sunrise, drift on houseboats at sunset. Home of the legendary Nehru Trophy Boat Race.

Open in Maps ↗
Waterfall · Trek ⭐ 4.5

Marmala Waterfalls

📍 ~42 km · Erattupetta

200 ft cascade deep in rubber jungle. A thrilling 2 km trek through spice-scented forest leads to the "Enchantress of the Jungle."

Open in Maps ↗
Theme Park ⭐ 4.3

Mango Meadows

📍 ~30 km · Kaduthuruthy

World's first agri-theme park. Archery, go-karts, VR cinema, boat rides, pot-making. A full-day family experience.

Open in Maps ↗
Waterfall ⭐ 4.1

Aruvikuzhy Waterfalls

📍 ~25 km · Pampady

Multi-tiered 30 ft cascade amid rubber plantations. Serene pathways and beautiful monsoon flows. Entry ~₹26.

Open in Maps ↗
Hidden Gem ⭐ 4.6

Kadavupuzha Aruvi

📍 ~36 km · Moonnilavu

A calm, family-safe waterfall with gentle cascades and lush surroundings. Rarely crowded, open 6:30 AM–6:30 PM.

Open in Maps ↗

🌿 Travel Tip — Most spots are 30–45 min from campus by road. July–September offers monsoon magic; October–March brings clear skies. Autos and taxis are available from IIIT Kottayam gate.
